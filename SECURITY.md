# Security Policy · Shipmate

Shipmate (`https://shipmate.agency`) takes security issues in our open-source tools, templates, and website seriously.

## Reporting a Vulnerability
- **Email:** [hello@shipmate.agency](mailto:hello@shipmate.agency)
- **Web:** https://shipmate.agency/contact
- **Response target:** Within 1 business day.

Please include reproduction steps, affected repository/commit, and expected vs actual behavior. Do not run destructive tests or access data you do not own.

## Scope & Safe Harbor
Good-faith research on repositories under `github.com/Shipmate-agency` and `https://shipmate.agency` is welcome. Third-party services and client environments are strictly out of scope.
